# Plan – Slice 04 "Full-suite lock"

Round R01 (field lessons) · Slice 04 · Model sonnet · Deps: 03 (done) · Integration branch `plugin-vibe-coding` · Slice branch `slice-04` in `.claude/worktrees/slice-04`

## Goal
Two slice sessions never run a full test suite at the same time: before a full suite `/slice` takes a lock under `.slice-loop/` in the main checkout and releases it afterwards; an orphaned lock is reported, never overwritten (F-01 lock, F-05).

## Assumptions (confirm on approval)
1. **Placement.** One paragraph "Full-suite lock" at the end of section 4 step 1 (the checks), applying also to the baseline check of section 2 when that is a full suite (section 2 gets a pointer of one clause). Recommendation: yes.
2. **Protocol.** Before a command that `work/conventions.md` names a full suite: `mkdir .slice-loop/full-suite.lock` in the main checkout (PowerShell: `New-Item -ItemType Directory` without `-Force`; both fail when the lock exists, so taking it is atomic), then write `owner` inside with `slice NN` and an ISO-8601 time; release it (remove the directory) right after the suite, red or green. Recommendation: yes.
3. **Documented limits.** Held by a live slice (its branch `slice-MM` exists and the time is younger than 60 minutes): wait and retry every minute, for at most 30 minutes. Otherwise (branch gone, older than 60 minutes, or the wait ran out) report it: in `auto` `SLICE-OPEN: NN lock held by slice MM since <time>`, else name owner and time to the user; leave the lock untouched. The numbers are mine: the longest suite seen in the field notes ran about 18 minutes. Recommendation: yes.
4. **Where the main checkout is.** The parent of `git rev-parse --git-common-dir`, as slice 03 defined for the message file; the folder `.slice-loop/` is created when missing and is already git-ignored. Recommendation: yes.
5. **This repository's own full suite** is `tests/loop_test.py` (conventions); the lock therefore guards that run, and the plan's own runs take it by hand. Recommendation: yes.
6. **`status`** shows one line for a held lock (owner, since when). Recommendation: yes.
7. **Templates and skill.** The conventions template line becomes `… never two full suites at once (the lock `.slice-loop/full-suite.lock/`, see /slice) …`; SKILL.md section 6 lists `.slice-loop/full-suite.lock/` with the git-ignored or local files. Recommendation: yes.
8. **Budget.** The command stands at 220 lines, the limit of `test_command_stays_short_and_positive`. The protocol needs about 8 lines (paragraph plus the status line), so the limit moves to 230 with the reason in its docstring; the "never" ceiling of 16 stays (the text says "leave untouched"). Recommendation: yes.
9. **Parallel slice 08** works in `plugins/slice-board/` plus checklist, round record and CHANGELOG. This slice does not touch `plugins/slice-board/`; before the squash the integration branch is merged into `slice-04` and the conflict checklist of section 4 step 6 is applied (resolving and committing in separate commands). Recommendation: yes.
10. **Tests** are phrase tests on the normalised text; privacy scan by hand; CHANGELOG bullet; no README change (slice 07). Recommendation: yes.
